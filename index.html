About

Who I Am

I am a quantum computing researcher and AI engineer pursuing an MSc in Computer Science at Daffodil International University, Dhaka. My research sits at the intersection of quantum information theory, entanglement-based communication, and quantum machine learning.

I believe effective teaching means simplifying complex technical concepts into practical, hands-on experiences. I guide learners to think critically, apply concepts through coding and experimentation, and build confidence in AI, ML, and quantum computing.

Currently authoring three manuscripts targeting IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering, covering covert quantum communication, quantum-repeater memory management, and multipartite entanglement certification.

What I've Built

🌀
EA-CQC Simulator
  • Closed-form capacity analysis: unassisted Gaussian vs. entanglement-assisted (TMSV) schemes over thermal-loss bosonic channels
  • Computes quantum relative entropy, QCB, finite-blocklength penalties, and Square Root Law scaling — no approximation
  • 21-invariant validation suite; accompanies an IEEE TQE manuscript
Python NumPy SciPy Matplotlib
📡
CTD: Quantum Repeater Buffer Simulator
  • Dual-threshold Congestion-Triggered Distillation policy for quantum-repeater entanglement memories under Poisson traffic
  • Three-way policy benchmark (Always-Distill / Never-Distill / CTD) across jitter-robustness and hardware-sensitivity scenarios
  • Demonstrates yield improvement and reduced memory-cutoff waste
Python NumPy Matplotlib Qiskit

GME Certification Tool
